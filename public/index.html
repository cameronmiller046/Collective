        Our Story

        Rise from what broke you. Build what sets you free.

        Coach Kia • Founder • Author • Life Coach • Visionary

        A guide for the whole journey, root to crown.

        “I help women rise from what broke them and build what sets them free.”

        Rooted & Crowned was born from the journey.

        Rooted & Crowned Collective was not created from theory alone. It was created from a life that required me to learn how to begin again—more than once.

        My journey has moved through love and loss, marriage and divorce, motherhood and advocacy, raising children with unique needs, rebuilding relationships, rediscovering myself, and learning that sometimes the life we planned and the life we are given are not the same.

        For years, I knew how to survive. But eventually, survival was no longer enough. I wanted to understand the patterns beneath my experiences, reconnect with the parts of myself I had lost along the way, and become intentional about the woman I was becoming.

        That journey became the foundation of Rooted & Crowned.

        I began looking at growth from the Root up—our foundation, creativity, personal power, relationships, voice, vision, and purpose. But I also realized that awareness could not be the destination. What we discover about ourselves must eventually become part of how we live.

        That became the heart of my philosophy:

        Heal from the Root Up. Live from the Crown Down.

        Rooted & Crowned Collective grew from that understanding—a space where we can acknowledge where we’ve been without allowing it to become the conclusion of our story. A place where reflection becomes action, our voices matter, and what once challenged us can become wisdom for what we build next.

        I don’t stand here as someone who has lived a perfect life. I stand here as a woman who has lived, learned, rebuilt, created, and continued becoming.

        And now I create spaces for other women to do the same.

        The journey became the framework.

        What began as my own search for understanding became a way of looking at growth differently: not as one moment of transformation, but as a journey from foundation to purpose—and from awareness into intentional living.

        The Root-to-Crown Framework

        Ground. Create. Empower. Love. Speak. See. Integrate.

        The Rooted & Crowned Framework is a seven-part journey of reflection, awareness, and intentional growth. Moving from Root to Crown, each stage invites you to explore a different part of your life—from the foundation that shaped you to the purpose guiding who you are becoming.

          The Method

          Heal from the Root Up. Live from the Crown Down.

          Root-up is the journey inward.

          We begin at the foundation—looking at the experiences, beliefs, patterns, relationships, emotions, and choices that have shaped us. We move upward with greater awareness, examining what we carry, what still serves us, and what we are ready to change.

          Crown-down is the journey forward.

          Awareness becomes meaningful when we begin living differently. From the Crown down, we bring purpose, vision, truth, love, personal power, creativity, and grounded intention into our everyday choices.

          This is where reflection becomes practice.

          Root ↑

          Heal Reflect Recognize Realign

          Crown ↓

          Envision Choose Create Live

          You don’t heal just to understand your past.
          You heal so you can become intentional about what you build next.

              The Rooted & Crowned App

              Your Daily Energy Companion

              Answer. Align. Elevate.

              Check in with how you're arriving today. Your answers point to a Root-to-Crown reflection theme with an insight, an affirmation, a prompt and a short practice. Over time, your journal and progress show the themes you return to.

              Try it: the preview on the right runs a real check-in. Tap through the questions and see which theme your answers point to.

              The Daily Loop
              • Check InEnergy, mood, grounding, creativity, confidence, heart, voice, perspective and purpose, on simple 1–5 scales. Two to four minutes.
              • ReflectYour answers suggest a theme worth exploring today. No labels, no scores to worry about.
              • ReceiveToday's Root-to-Crown insight: one short insight, one affirmation, one reflection prompt, one practice.
              • PracticeGrounding, breathing, journaling, mindfulness, affirmations, boundaries, voice work, gratitude, intention setting.
              • TrackCheck-ins, the themes you explore most, practices completed and self-reported mood and energy over time.
              • ConnectWhen you're ready to go deeper: the 7-Week Experience, Journal Club, books, Conversations and community.
